# Titles, descriptions and hashtags

Write in the language of the video (German if unclear). Never promise something the clip
does not deliver: misleading hooks kill retention and trust.

## YouTube Shorts title (`shorts_title`)
- 40-70 characters, max 100. The installer's text file appends `#shorts`.
- Curiosity or benefit up front: "Warum Oktopusse nicht gern schwimmen", "Der größte Spar-Fehler".
- At most one emoji, no ALL CAPS sentences (one caps word for emphasis is fine).
- No clickbait the clip cannot pay off; no names of people who are not in the clip.

## TikTok description (`tiktok_description`)
- 1-2 sentences, ideally under 150 characters before the hashtags.
- Restate the hook in other words or tease the payoff; end with a question or call to
  comment ("Wusstest du das?", "Welche Regel hilft dir?").
- For Creative Commons sources the export appends the credit line automatically; do not
  remove it.

## Hashtags (`hashtags`)
- 4-7 tags: 2-3 specific to the topic (#oktopus #meeresbiologie), 1-2 niche/community
  (#wissenschaftsfakten), 1-2 broad (#fyp or #lernenmittiktok). No banned or unrelated
  trending tags, no more than 8.
- German content: prefer German tags; add an English broad tag only if the topic is international.

## Posting plan (automatic)
`finalize` orders clips: strongest first (it sets the reach for the rest), then alternates
topics so two clips from the same source moment are not posted back to back; one clip per
day at 18:00 on weekdays and 12:00 on weekends. Adjust in `posting_plan.csv` if the user's
analytics show other peak times.
