# Virality scoring rubric (0-100 each)

The final score = 0.25 hook + 0.20 retention + 0.20 emotion + 0.15 entertainment + 0.20 virality,
minus QA penalties (4 per remaining warning, 15 per remaining failure). Clips under
`min_virality` (default 55) are dropped unless fewer than 5 clips would remain.

Calibrate against the whole internet, not against the other clips of this video. Most clips
of an ordinary video land between 45 and 75. Reserve 85+ for clips you would bet on.

## hook - first 1-3 seconds
- 90-100: the first sentence alone makes you stay: a shocking claim, a number that sounds
  wrong, open conflict, a strong question, a visible emotional peak. No warm-up.
- 70-89: clear, interesting opener, but needs a second sentence to bite.
- 50-69: starts mid-topic, neutral tone; viewer has to wait for the point.
- < 50: greeting, filler ("so, yeah"), context-dependent opener ("as I said").

## retention - will people watch to the end?
- 90-100: open loop or tension that pays off at the very end; every sentence adds.
- 70-89: steady, no dead stretch; payoff a bit early.
- 50-69: one slow passage, repetition, or the payoff is in the middle.
- < 50: rambling, long setup, loses the thread.

## emotion
Laughter, surprise, anger, awe, empathy, nostalgia, tension. Score the intensity a viewer
feels, not what the speaker says they feel.

## entertainment
Humour, storytelling, personality, energy of delivery, visual interest. Educational clips score
here through clarity and "aha" moments.

## virality - shareability
- 90-100: people will send it to a friend or comment to argue/agree; quotable line; strong
  identity ("this is so us"), useful tip people save.
- 70-89: relatable or useful, likely to be liked/saved.
- 50-69: fine to watch, little reason to share.
- < 50: niche or needs knowledge of the channel.

## Hard drops regardless of score
- Not understandable without the original video.
- Ends mid-sentence or before the payoff.
- Face visibly cut off for most of the clip, or captions unreadable / misspelled names.
- Duplicate of a stronger clip (same point, same story).
- Misleading when taken out of context (could misrepresent what the person meant).
